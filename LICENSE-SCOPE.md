# Open release and rights scope

OpenAlterEgo is intended to let anyone study, modify, build, use, and sell an
independent implementation. The project's own material is released as follows:

| Material | License |
| --- | --- |
| `software/`, including firmware and experiment scripts added there | [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE-SOFTWARE) |
| Original project-authored documentation outside `hardware/` | [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE-SOFTWARE) |
| `hardware/` design sources and project-authored hardware documentation | [CERN Open Hardware Licence 2, Permissive](LICENSE-HARDWARE) |

The full license texts are included in this repository. Both licenses contain
contributor patent grants with defined scope and conditions. They apply only to
patent claims a contributor can license under the terms of the respective
license. They do not grant rights in patents held by unrelated third parties or
establish freedom to operate for a particular product. Older releases under MIT
remain available under their original terms; this release does not revoke them.
The Python distribution also carries copies of the Apache text and project
notice under `software/python/` so wheels and source distributions include them.

The literature PDFs, external datasets, upstream code, cited papers, names, and
trademarks in this repository are not relicensed by this statement. Consult
their original sources and terms before redistributing or using them. In
particular, publishing a benchmark result does not transfer rights to its data.

Hardware openness requires editable schematic, PCB, firmware, and mechanical
sources together with enough build information to reproduce a design. Current
specifications are research targets; the repository does not yet contain a
complete wearable design or imply that one has been tested on people.

Contributors should submit only material they have authority to license under
these terms and identify third-party components and data in their change.
